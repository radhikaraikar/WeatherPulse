IMD Impact-Based Warning Guidance & Sector Action Protocols
Public & Citizens
  • Avoid driving through waterlogged underpasses.
  • Stay away from electric poles, fallen wires, and transformers.
  • Keep emergency kit (torch, power bank, dry rations, medicines) ready.
Agriculture & Agromet
  • Provide adequate drainage in standing paddy, sugarcane, and pulses.
  • Suspend fertilizer top-dressing and chemical pesticide spraying.
  • Provide mechanical staking support to young fruit trees and banana crops.
Coastal & Marine Fishermen
  • Complete prohibition of fishing operations in Arabian Sea / Bay of Bengal.
  • Small craft and mechanized vessels must return to safe harbour docks.
  • Monitor INCOIS swell surge and tidal wave bulletins.
Aviation & Transport
  • Activate CAT-II/III Instrument Landing System (ILS) for fog runways.
  • Railways: Enforce speed restrictions on bridges over swelling rivers.
  • Highways: Deploy rapid clearing cranes for landslide-prone mountain passes.
